# Motion Storyboard — Tensura Abyss

| # | Chapter | Pattern | Scroll range | Transition | Title treatment |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Breach | Pinned Hero | 240vh | Crane descent | Outline split |
| 2 | Contract | Editorial release | 90vh | Natural cut | Split line |
| 3 | Evolution | Scrubbed timeline | 320vh | Fade through obsidian | Scale-down |
| 4 | World | Parallax gallery | Flow | Silver match cut | Editorial rise |
| 5 | Gear | Product reveal | 110vh | Radial push-in | Word reveal |
| 6 | Atomic | Symmetric monument | 100vh | Resolution | Outline lockup |

Desktop uses no more than six hero depth layers and two pinned chapters. A free-scrolling release separates them. Mobile removes all pinning, displays evolution cards in normal flow, retains one transform-only gear parallax mover, and uses one-shot entrance reveals. Reduced motion is fully static.

Timing follows a 1.2-second/100vh baseline. Simultaneous motion stays below three types: depth translation, scale, and opacity. No filter or layout property is animated.
