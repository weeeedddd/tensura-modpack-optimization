# Art Direction — Tensura Abyss

## World Premise
A violet crystal monolith hangs over a black grid while four secret bloodlines descend through an inhabited abyss.

## Hero Concept
The visitor descends past a Magicule Spire made of faceted violet light, delivered by six-layer scroll-coupled depth. It is brand-specific because the spire becomes the update's exclusive crafting resource.

## Motif System
1. Concentric seals — hero, gear, ultimate.
2. Faceted spires — hero, biome, crafting.
3. Silver hairlines — UI, safehouses, data caps.
4. Violet pressure fields — biome cards, blade, atomic charge.

## Material and Light
Obsidian absorbs; silver defines; violet energy leaks through seams; cyan appears only for the ultimate and safe-state signals.

## Assets
All visuals are procedural CSS in the self-contained page. No external image, font, video, or JavaScript asset is required.

## Signature Moment
At the hero's final third, the spire rises through six independently moving depth planes behind the outlined word “Evolve.”

## Wow Rubric
Hero 2 · motif 2 · motion-as-narrative 2 · depth 2 · material 2 · signature 2 = 12/12.
