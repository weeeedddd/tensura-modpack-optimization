TENSURA ABYSS — SHADOW GARDEN SYSTEM
=====================================

Architecture
------------
The companion mod owns registered items, armor materials, races, guild screens,
the server-authoritative Atomic cast, and live Tensura storage access. KubeJS
owns recipes, ritual/evolution gates, raids, world interaction, and chat format.

Core progression
----------------
1. Choose one of four nine-stage race paths.
2. Meet native EP gates plus the Cult, catalyst, and Shadow Abyss milestones.
3. Mine Magicule Spire Crystal inside the Shadow Abyss.
4. Refine Condensed Dark Matter and Abyssal Netherite Ingots.
5. Upgrade Netherite gear into capacity-resonant Abyssal Netherite.

I Am Atomic
-----------
Atomic is not craftable and consumes no item. Only the active race
`tensura_abyss:eminence_of_the_abyss` can cast it through Tensura's native
Race/Mount Ability key (default R). The server consumes 35% of maximum
Magicules, charges for two seconds, damages hostile entities in a 30-block
field with distance falloff, never damages blocks, and applies a 120-second
race cooldown.

Race traits
-----------
Every custom race gains Night Vision and Speed below light level 7. Stages 5–9
gain capped damage absorption and a small evasion chance. The four paths use
different balanced health, attack, movement, and toughness curves; the secret
race is strong but no longer grants four-digit bonus health.

Abyssal Netherite
------------------
Required IDs:
  tensura_abyss:magicule_spire_crystal
  tensura_abyss:condensed_dark_matter
  tensura_abyss:abyssal_netherite_ingot
  tensura_abyss:abyssal_netherite_sword
  tensura_abyss:abyssal_netherite_<helmet|chestplate|leggings|boots>

The sword scales to +8 attack damage. A full armor set scales to +6 armor and
+4 toughness. Both curves use live maximum Magicule capacity and hard caps.

Guild presentation
------------------
Guild and market screens share an obsidian, violet, and silver palette. Team
prefixes remain blank; ShadowSightHandler renders one viewer-gated nameplate
badge, and shadow_chat.js emits one compact chat prefix for faction members.

Deployment
----------
Build the companion mod from `dev/companion-mod` with `gradlew.bat clean build`,
then include its jar and the `overrides/` tree in the modpack export. Restart is
required after registry changes; server scripts can be hot-reloaded afterward.
